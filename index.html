⏳

1. Bi-Temporal SCD2

Preserves transaction posting time (_ingest_ts) and business validity reality (_valid_from_ts, _valid_to_ts) with zero destructive overwrites.

💵

2. Absolute Currency Precision

Floating-point IEEE-754 numbers (FLOAT, DOUBLE) are strictly prohibited. All financial columns mandate DECIMAL(18,2) to pass SOX audits.

🔐

3. Deterministic SHA-256 Diff

Replaces expensive 30-column SQL OR equality joins during MERGE INTO with a single 64-character SHA-256 payload hash comparison.

⭐

4. Conformed Kimball Star

Gold fact tables never join on natural keys. They join strictly on monotonic integer surrogate keys (customer_sk, product_sk) for columnar performance.

🏷️

5. Semantic Suffix Taxonomy

Strict column suffix conventions (_sk, _id, _amt, _cnt, _cd, _flg, _dt, _ts) enable automated governance catalogs.

🛡️

6. 14 Mandatory Audit Fields

Every Silver and Gold table embeds standard audit lineage columns capturing source systems, ingestion timestamps, pipeline UUIDs, and DQ scores.

Regulatory Crosswalk & Lakehouse Governance Matrix

                
How Medallion architecture patterns enforce strict enterprise compliance mandates.


                

                    

                        

                            SOX 404 (Financial Internal Controls)
                            SOX
                        

                        

                            Requires mathematical reproducibility and full auditability of all financial statements. 
                            Enforced by banning floating-point types (DECIMAL(18,2) mandatory) and preserving the complete lineage envelope from Bronze ingest to Gold general ledger marts.
                        

                    


                    

                        

                            GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure)
                            GDPR
                        

                        

                            In append-only Parquet/Delta architectures, deleting individual user rows from hundreds of historical partitions is computationally prohibitive.
                            Enforced via Crypto-Shredding: PII columns are encrypted with a per-customer key (_gdpr_crypto_key_id). On erasure, deleting the encryption key renders historical PII permanently unrecoverable.
                        

                    


                    

                        

                            SOC 2 Type II (Security & Availability)
                            SOC 2
                        

                        

                            Requires proof of continuous data integrity and execution logging.
                            Enforced by embedding orchestration pipeline UUIDs (_etl_job_id, _pipeline_run_id) and SHA-256 payload validation hashes into every row.
                        

                    


                    

                        

                            GAAP / ASC 606 (Revenue Recognition)
                            GAAP
                        

                        

                            Mandates precise recognition of subscription contract revenue over time without double counting.
                            Enforced by non-overlapping Bi-Temporal SCD2 validity ranges and conformed date dimension surrogate joins.
